How to actually protect yourself

  • Check the domain before you click — this page lives at a domain built to look suspicious on purpose.
  • Unexpected prizes, QR codes on flyers/parking meters, and "urgent" links are the classic bait.
  • Device and browser info leaks to any site you visit — that part isn't avoidable, just don't hand over more (email, location, passwords) to sites you don't trust.
  • When in doubt, type the company's real URL yourself instead of tapping a link or QR code.
